# Affiliation review — 25 September 2026

Reviewed all 77 roster entries and their recorded affiliation rationales for category errors, unsupported overlaps, and misleading display labels. Fresh source checks focused on Mill, the rights/social-contract grouping, Nussbaum, consequentialist scope, and mixed or retrospective affiliations. The register below records the rationale and source for every retained membership; it does not claim that every linked source received a fresh full-text audit or that each person's complete philosophy is exhausted by these categories.

## Corrections

- **John Stuart Mill:** removed the secondary rights-based membership. Political liberalism does not establish an independent rights-based ethical foundation. His moral framework remains utilitarian. [SEP, Mill](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/mill/).
- **Martha Nussbaum:** moved the primary affiliation from virtue ethics to a separate capabilities category. Her own account identifies capabilities as the normative framework; Aristotelian influence is not sufficient to equate the two theories. Retained her independent political-liberal/entitlements affiliation. [Nussbaum's Holberg lecture](https://www.law.uchicago.edu/news/justice-animals-practical-progress-through-philosophical-theory), [SEP, Capability Approach](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/capability-approach/).
- **Category labels:** the rights grouping is now Rights & social-contract ethics, rather than a catch-all for political liberals. The short labels Utilitarian / consequentialist and Aristotelian / virtue now preserve distinctions already present in their full definitions. Existing category IDs remain stable for shared links.

## Borderline and mixed cases retained

- Beccaria's public-utility membership and contested social-contract overlap remain separate. Unlike an inference from liberal policy alone, his work expressly invokes the social contract and limits on sovereign power. [Primary excerpt](https://constitutioncenter.org/the-constitution/historic-document-library/detail/cesare-bonesana-di-beccariaon-crimes-and-punishments-1764).
- Rousseau remains in the social-contract branch, not labeled a straightforward modern liberal. Voltaire's membership rests on the recorded natural-rights argument; Paine, Locke, Wollstonecraft and Salt likewise have substantive rights arguments. Rawls's justice as fairness supports rights/social-contract classification; his Kantian construction is separately qualified. [Rousseau](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/rousseau/), [Voltaire](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/voltaire/), [Rawls](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/rawls/).
- Russell, Parfit and Ord are retained in the broad consequentialist family without implying unwavering classical utilitarianism. Russell's primary humanist affiliation remains. Ord explicitly documents a defense of global consequentialism. [Ord](https://www.tobyord.com/research), [Parfit obituary](https://www.philosophy.ox.ac.uk/files/obituary-derekparfitpdf).
- Bostrom, Greaves and MacAskill are included for substantial framework use in the recorded arguments, not inferred exclusive personal allegiance. Their conditional utilitarian premises are disclosed. Effective-altruist or longtermist association by itself is not an admission criterion. [Bostrom's primary argument](https://nickbostrom.com/papers/astronomical-waste/), [Greaves's research description](https://users.ox.ac.uk/~mert2255/).
- Nietzsche and Kierkegaard retain explicitly retrospective precursor classifications. Camus's rejected label and Jonas's background connection remain contested; neither becomes core through the Include contested control. [Existentialism](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/existentialism/), [Camus](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/camus/).
- Christian/Stoic (Lipsius, du Vair), Christian/Aristotelian (Aquinas), Islamic/Aristotelian (Ibn Rushd), Catholic/virtue (Anscombe), and existentialist/Marxist (Sartre) memberships describe documented substantive overlaps. Fichte is a post-Kantian development, not orthodox Kant; Cohen is Neo-Kantian. These qualifications stay in the records.
- Religious affiliations refer to substantive arguments and lineages, not birthplace or nominal religion. Ambedkar's Buddhist classification is confined to the late Buddhist work represented here. Bible and Quran are collections, not individual authors; the Bible's Christian display grouping does not make the Hebrew Bible's authors Christian.

No further membership changes were justified by this review. The taxonomy remains an editorial grouping of documented frameworks, not mutually exclusive psychological identities. Adding missing affiliations requires positive evidence; an association or a similar policy conclusion is insufficient.
