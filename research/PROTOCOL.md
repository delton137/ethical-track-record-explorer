# Ethical Track Record Explorer — research protocol

## Unit of evidence

The unit is a person's dated documented position, not their life, conduct, followers, influence on legislation, or moral worth. Author-authenticated letters, manuscripts, treatises and signed texts qualify. Qualified transmitted teachings may also qualify when the attributed speaker, reporter, transmission chain and inspected witness are disclosed. They must be labeled reported teachings, not authored works; a report does not establish an autograph or verbatim oral record. Translations and scholarly transcriptions are primary-text witnesses, but not autographs. Quotation status describes the witness actually checked; it does not claim that every manuscript variant has been collated.

Select candidate authors by intellectual importance and substantive ethical affiliation before scoring. Preserve religious branches and mixed or disputed membership. An author using a framework substantially can qualify without exclusive allegiance. A source discussing a framework does not by itself prove personal adherence. Affiliation and importance evidence must be displayed independently of position evidence.

## Checklist applied to every figure

Search for slavery; racial equality and colonial self-determination; women's legal equality; women's suffrage; religious toleration; same-sex decriminalization; marriage equality; torture; executions; protection of children; animal welfare; farmed-animal welfare; human extinction; wild-animal welfare; insect welfare; and AI welfare. Review the surrounding primary passage for exceptions, quoted opponents, irony, and revisions. Search for contrary writings. Unknown is not opposition or agreement. The generated checklist records what is supported and what remains unresolved; absence of an entry must never become a score.

## Dates, editions and quotations

Keep composition and first publication separate. If the exact composition date cannot be securely determined, use a declared conservative publication-year proxy rather than inventing an earlier date. Keep the work’s first publication and the inspected edition date separate from the passage’s dated publication witness. Do not label a revised edition as the work’s first publication or backdate a passage without evidence. Contemporary attestation of the exact wording can establish an earlier date. Public-only comparisons use the passage publication date. A private position can be excluded even when eventually published. Ranges represent genuine uncertainty. For ancient works without securely dated publication, disclose the work-period circulation proxy rather than implying a known first edition. For reported teachings, keep the attributed teaching period, earliest identified textual attestation and modern witness/translation date separate; public-only calculations use the declared attestation proxy. Unknown birth and death years can be replaced by a sourced floruit uncertainty envelope, not invented life endpoints. Page numbers refer to the named edition, or explicitly to PDF/typescript pagination. Use an accessible primary text or faithful scholarly transcription. Record the translator and any unresolved translator identification. OCR with a potentially material error stays qualified until checked against the page image. Never repair an uncertain passage silently.

Use short excerpts (normally under 25 words from each work across the shipped corpus), with editorial context and an outbound source. Do not assign a quotation made by an author's opponent to the author. Preserve qualification and material counterevidence. Generic future-generation concern is insufficient for human-extinction concern. Generic concern for life is insufficient to establish insect sentience or a specific wild-welfare intervention.

## Benchmarks

Historical windows are named, limited reference cases, not dates on which the whole West agreed. Match the proposition to the reform: anti-cruelty is not veganism, toleration is not universal suffrage, and decriminalization is not marriage equality. The 1911 animal-protection benchmark uses enactment (commencement was 1912), covers England, Wales and Ireland, and excludes Scotland. The alternative 1822 livestock benchmark has narrower scope and is a sensitivity comparison, not an equivalent universal protection. Benchmark-dependent rankings must not be interpreted as dates of general social agreement. Treat the upward arc as the project's normative assumption. Extinction 2000–2030 and wild-animal welfare statutes including insects 2060–2100 are stipulated windows. AI welfare statutes 2040–2100 and the factory-farming / veganism transition 2000–2100 are hypothetical scenarios. All are excluded from historical ranks, even if part of their window is in the past.

## Computation

For a writing interval [w0,w1] and adoption interval [a0,a1], support scores [max(a0-w1,0), max(a1-w0,0)]; opposition uses f(d) = −0.5d for d > 0 and f(d) = d otherwise, where d is adoption minus writing. Evaluate both endpoints of [a0-w1,a1-w0] for the score range, including zero as its maximum if the interval contains zero. Ambiguous and unmatched positions are unscored. Display the interval midpoint only as a plotting convention. Average distinct episodes within each figure/domain, people with equal fractional qualifying affiliation weights within tradition/domain, then equally weight covered historical domains. Require at least 3 core figures with scored historical evidence and 3 domains for an overall rank. Importance affects line thickness only. Duplicate quotations and records sharing an episode ID never add weight.

## Historical dates and compressed display

The full view defaults to 800 BCE–2100 to include the selected biblical composition ranges. Internal years use astronomical numbering (1 = 1 CE, 0 = 1 BCE, −399 = 400 BCE); displayed historical labels have no year zero. Bare positive years in controls and existing links remain CE. Date controls accept BCE/BC and CE/AD. Historical filtering and score arithmetic use actual years, including uncertainty endpoints. Future-scenario validation remains separate, restricted to 1400–2500.

The Ancient section covers dates before 1500 and reserves approximately 160 pixels in the full view. Occupied intervals combine all included figures’ documented pre-1500 lifetimes/floruit and composition/publication intervals, padded by 25 years; gaps of at least 200 years become narrow marked breaks. A range crossing a gap protects its entire interval. Geometry comes from the full corpus, so searching people or changing issue, tradition, evidence or public-only filters does not move surviving dates. Changing the displayed period or viewport intentionally changes geometry. Breaks disclose omitted date ranges on hover and keyboard focus; exports and `/api/research` expose the segments and protected intervals. Connection lines omit the same break strips. Compression never enters the scoring or ranking functions.

The progress reference rises by 24 display pixels from 800 BCE through 1700. The modern section retains its prior 1500–1700 horizontal scale and 25-degree rise after 1700, with existing future scenarios. All dots, writing uncertainty endpoints, connections, benchmark endpoints and exported placements use the same axis mapping. Vertical distance is schematic, not a uniform numerical score scale.

## Audit and limitations

Every position has an explicit counterevidence note. A note that research remains open is not a completed scholarly audit. The first release is an inspectable research corpus, not a census or causal demonstration of a philosophy's effects. Coverage differences, survivorship, translation availability, selection of Western benchmarks, and the chosen starting period limit comparisons. Candidate records, when present, must be visibly separated and never plotted or scored. Unadmitted research leads are listed separately in CANDIDATES.md. Export includes the exact filters, benchmarks, scenario settings, source data, calculations and algorithm version needed to reproduce the view.


## Affiliation review — 24 September 2026

Reviewed the corpus affiliation records for primary/core consistency, mixed membership, and retrospective classifications. Core membership means documented framework use, not exclusive allegiance or nominal religious identity. Broad families such as consequentialism, rights/social-contract ethics, and Aristotelian virtue ethics retain their explicit qualifications. The 25 September review separates capabilities from virtue ethics and removes Mill’s rights-based membership; see [AFFILIATIONS.md](AFFILIATIONS.md) for the complete roster review.

Nietzsche and Kierkegaard are core in the broad existentialist family as foundational nineteenth-century precursors. This recognizes their substantive contribution without implying self-identification or membership in the later twentieth-century movement. Kierkegaard's Christian affiliation also remains core. See the [SEP history of existentialism](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/existentialism/). Both now appear in default existentialist filtering and comparisons; individual position scores are unchanged. Camus remains contested given his rejection of the label; Jonas's distinct ethics of responsibility remains a qualified, contested existentialist connection.

Russell is core in both secular humanism and the broad utilitarian/consequentialist family. His [*Elements of Ethics*, III §§16–18](https://fair-use.org/bertrand-russell/the-elements-of-ethics/section-iii) makes probable consequences the criterion of objective rightness. The [IEP account](https://iep.utm.edu/russ-eth/) distinguishes this continuity from changes in his theory of value and metaethics. Requiring an exclusive utilitarian identity would contradict this corpus’s stated admission rule. This does not classify him as an unwavering classical hedonistic utilitarian; secular humanism remains his primary display affiliation.

The Include contested control governs tradition filtering, legend counts, display colors, and comparison contributions. It does not promote contested affiliations to core status for ranking eligibility. All-traditions mode retains contested-only authors as neutral-colored records when disabled. When enabled, a selected eligible affiliation supplies the color; otherwise the core primary affiliation takes precedence, followed by another core affiliation, then a contested affiliation. Research-index cards explicitly distinguish core and contested memberships.


## Scriptural collections — 24 September 2026

The Bible and Quran are admitted as named scriptural collections using the same figure, search, filter, connection and episode-weighting model as people. They are labeled `scripture` with `scriptural-text` attribution, and have composition envelopes rather than invented lifetimes. A collection contributes one figure per covered tradition/domain; it is not evidence that all adherents endorse each passage. Connections identify collection membership, not a single author’s biography or a proved sequence of doctrinal changes.

Dates attach to the relevant book or surah, not to the event narrated or the English translation. Biblical ranges are qualified composition/redaction and circulation proxies. Quranic composition uses broad Meccan/Medinan proxies (610–622 / 622–632 CE, or 610–632 for mixed material); public-only dates use the conventional Uthmanic codification envelope, 644–656 CE. This is not a claim of verse-specific surviving manuscripts from that period.

Public-domain KJV and Pickthall excerpts may exceed the normal 25-word aggregate per-work budget only when every quoted witness of that work has an explicit `publicDomainUrl` rights record. Quotations remain brief, linked and contextualized; the exception does not cover modern translations or host commentary.

Unmatched scripture passages remain in the evidence table but are not plotted or connected. The five matched Bible points and three matched Quran points form compact groups with 12-pixel vertical spacing, anchored just below the reference at each text’s earliest plotted date. Hover labels are spaced independently. Their x dates and computed scores do not change; exported baseY and displayOffsetY record the display offset. Afterlife promises are not coded as denial of biological extinction risk; anti-infanticide and Sabbath-rest passages are not coded as child-labor abolition. Moral condemnation without a human criminal penalty is not automatically equivalent to opposition to decriminalization. See SCRIPTURES.md for the passage register and dating sources.

## Comparisons and postdictions — 25 September 2026

Comparisons always exclude supportive historical positions whose selected writing-date midpoint is strictly after the effective benchmark end. Public-only mode uses publication dates; alternative benchmarks use their selected endpoint. This exclusion happens before episode deduplication, averaging, domain coverage, core-figure eligibility and counts. Opposition remains eligible; a writing midpoint exactly at the endpoint is not a postdiction. The Show postdictions control changes the chart and evidence table, never Comparisons. Provisional/unmatched records remain separately reported as unscored, and editable future scenarios cannot change comparison scores or coverage. Exported historicalLeaderboard uses the same comparison function as the UI.

### Ethical foresight scoring

Ethical foresight is the sole rule for all numerical scores: opposition receives −0.5 times the years before reform, or −1 times the years after reform; opposition at reform scores zero. Support scores max(adoption − writing, 0). These units are weighted years, with an editorial half-weight for early opposition. For uncertain dates, evaluate the penalty at both extremes of benchmark minus writing; include zero as the maximum when the difference interval crosses zero. Display the resulting score-range midpoint. Historical-only comparisons, postdiction exclusion, episode deduplication, affiliation weights and coverage thresholds remain the same. Exports describe this rule; there is no scoring-mode setting.
