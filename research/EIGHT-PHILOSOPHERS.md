# Eight-philosopher dossier review — 24 September 2026

This expansion added eight figures and eleven documented positions. All eleven carry material qualifications. Nine receive existing benchmark matches; two remain unscored. These are passage-level reviews, not complete collected-works audits. Short quotations, locators, attribution, dating sources and contrary evidence are stored in `eight-philosophers.ts` and exposed in the evidence panel and API. The [subsequent review](SIDGWICK-RUSSELL-STOICS.md) adds four Sidgwick positions and two ancient Stoics. See [coverage.json](coverage.json) for the current roster.

The ancient composition intervals also serve as declared circulation proxies where independent first-circulation dates are unavailable. Musonius is treated differently because the admission is explicitly a transmitted report: public-only comparisons use textual attestation rather than the attributed teaching date. Dates of inspected modern translations are never substituted for ancient composition dates.

Per-work excerpt budgets are enforced over unique stored quotations, combining separate question pages and shared passages. Broader slavery/women searches remain incomplete for every figure lacking direct evidence; generic non-injury, virtuous character or common humanity does not generate additional reform scores.

The full-corpus Ancient geometry includes pre-1500 life/floruit ranges even for Renaissance authors whose surviving positions are later. It protects both composition and public-date uncertainty, adds 25-year padding, and compresses only gaps of at least 200 years. All actual years continue to determine scores and historical rankings; the expanded corpus can change aggregate rankings through new evidence, never through compressed display coordinates.
